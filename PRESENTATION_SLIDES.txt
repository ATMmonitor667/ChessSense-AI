SLIDE 6 — THE MODEL (UNDER THE HOOD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TITLE:
    Specialized model — not “chat” as the brain

CONTENT:
    • Input: fixed-size encoding (piece planes + globals + move one-hot —
      aligns Python training + TypeScript inference).
    • Output: 5 logits → softmax → grade bucket + peak confidence.
    • Trained labels mirror the same heuristic rules as the web grade (explicit
      trade-off documented).

SPEAKER HOOK ONE-LINER:

     labels that match our product logic."
